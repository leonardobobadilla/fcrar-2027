About the conference

A Florida robotics tradition since 1988

FCRAR gives graduate and undergraduate students an accessible venue to present research, exchange ideas, and connect with faculty and industry. The 2027 edition will be hosted by Florida International University in Miami.

The program is planned to include oral presentations, invited talks, an industry panel, robotics competitions and demonstrations, and FIU laboratory tours.
