Call for papers

Author information

FCRAR welcomes research papers related to robotics from students, faculty, and collaborators across academic and applied settings.

Paper submissions

Submit papers in IEEE conference format, with a maximum length of eight pages. There is no fee to submit or publish a paper. Accepted papers will be published as open-access proceedings through Digital Commons at the University of South Florida.

Technical topics

Topics include, but are not limited to:

  • Robot vision
  • Learning and adaptation
  • Cognitive robotics
  • Cooperation and competition
  • Robot SLAM
  • Assistive robotics
  • Human–robot interaction
  • Rehabilitation robotics
  • Robot kinematics and dynamics
  • Robot operating systems
  • Control theory
  • Simulation and visualization
  • Mechatronics
  • Robot swarms
  • Navigation and path planning
  • Biologically inspired robots
  • Robot manipulators
  • Embedded hardware
  • Humanoid robots
  • Robotic applications
  • Micro and nano robots
  • Unmanned aerial robots
  • Automation and teleoperation
  • Underwater robotic systems
  • Multi-robot systems
  • Sensors and sensor fusion
  • Search and rescue robots
  • Educational robotics
